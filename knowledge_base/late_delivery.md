# Late delivery

Category: late_delivery
Allowed resolutions: shipping_refund, store_credit
Window: 30 days from delivery

## Policy

If an order is delivered at least 5 days after the promised delivery date,
the customer may choose a refund of the shipping cost or a 10% store credit.

- The order must have been delivered at least 5 days late. A delivery that
  is on time or less than 5 days late does not qualify.
- A full refund of the order and a replacement are NOT available for a late
  delivery: the item was delivered, so there is nothing to replace and the
  order itself is not refundable on lateness alone.
- The request must be made within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Store credit is applied to the customer's account after review and
  approval; it is not instant.

## What the agent must not say

Never tell the customer a refund has been issued or credit has been applied.
The agent only records a request for review.
