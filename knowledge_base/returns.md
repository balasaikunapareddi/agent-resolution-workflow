# Returns (general)

Category: returns
Allowed resolutions: refund, store_credit
Window: 30 days from delivery

## Policy

A customer may return an item within 30 days of delivery for a refund to
the original payment method or store credit.

- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
- Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are not accepted.
- A refund is issued only after the return is received and reviewed,
  normally within 5-10 business days after the return arrives.
- Store credit is applied to the customer's account after review and
  approval; it is not instant.
- A replacement is NOT available under the general returns policy. If the
  item is damaged, defective, or the wrong item, use that policy instead.

## What the agent must not say

Never tell the customer a refund has been issued or credit has been applied.
The agent only records a request for review.
