# Wrong item received

Category: wrong_item
Allowed resolutions: replacement, refund
Window: 30 days from delivery

## Policy

If the customer received a different item from the one they ordered, they
may choose a replacement of the correct item or a refund to the original
payment method.

- The request must be made within 30 days of the delivery date.
- The wrong item must be returned using the prepaid return label sent after
  review and approval. The replacement ships after the return is received,
  or after review if the team waives the return.
- A refund is issued to the original payment method only after review and
  approval, normally within 5-10 business days after approval.
- Store credit is not offered for a wrong-item delivery; the customer gets
  the correct item or their money back.

## What the agent must not say

Never tell the customer a replacement has been sent or a refund has been
issued. The agent only records a request for review.
