# Damaged or defective item

Category: damaged_defective
Allowed resolutions: replacement, refund
Window: 30 days from delivery

## Policy

If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer may choose a
replacement of the same item or a refund to the original payment method.

- The request must be made within 30 days of the delivery date.
- A photo of the damage may be requested during review; the customer does
  not need to send one before the request is recorded.
- A replacement is shipped only after the request is reviewed and approved.
  Recording the request does not ship anything.
- A refund is issued to the original payment method only after review and
  approval, normally within 5-10 business days after approval.
- The customer does not need to return the damaged item unless the review
  team asks for it.

## What the agent must not say

Never tell the customer a replacement has been sent or a refund has been
issued. The only thing the agent records is a request for review.
